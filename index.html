🧪 Interactive Labs

Hands-on benches and simulators. Nothing here is multiple choice — you work a ticket, terminate a run, tune a rule, and find out whether it held.

Interactive Labs
  • A+ Core 1 Field Service Center Generated service calls for 220-1201. Diagnose the fault, order the right part, follow the procedure, and prove the repair — hardware, laptops, printers, displays, cabling, mobile and cloud.
  • A+ Core 2 Field Service Center The same bench for 220-1202 — Windows and operating systems, security, software troubleshooting and operational procedures. Generated service calls and recall drills across all thirty-six sub-objectives, with the Windows dialogs drawn rather than described, and a 3D bench for safety, the server room and disposal, where the evidence is a thing you walk up to.
  • Patch Bay Terminate and trace runs across six topologies — star, mesh, bus, ring, tree and hybrid — then troubleshoot them from a Windows or Linux command line, spanning tree included.
  • OSI Model Lab Follow data down the seven layers and back up. Encapsulation, what each layer adds, and which protocols and ports live where.
  • Subnet Calculator Work subnets by hand and check yourself — CIDR prefixes, network and broadcast addresses, wildcard masks, usable ranges and VLSM.
  • IPv6 & EUI-64 Drills Compress and expand addresses, build an interface ID from a MAC, and tell link-local from global from solicited-node multicast.
  • Security Awareness Center The end-user side of the job — phishing, social engineering, passwords and MFA, physical security, and knowing what to report and when.
  • CySA CVSS Center One intrusion through seven tools. Score the vector, read firewall, SIEM, SOAR and EDR, tune the correlation rule that surfaces it, and account for what happened.
